Excited to share my latest end-to-end Data Analytics project: **Banking Customer, Transaction & Loan Analytics using SQL (MySQL 8+)**! 🚀📊

As a BBA Business Analytics graduate, I wanted to build a realistic, enterprise-grade project that goes far beyond basic tutorial datasets and demonstrates how advanced SQL converts complex banking transactions into strategic business intelligence.

---

### 📌 Project Objective:
Design a robust 7-table relational banking database and solve 38+ critical business questions covering customer demographics, wealth segmentation, channel migration, branch performance, and loan portfolio default risks.

---

### 🛠️ Tech Stack & SQL Techniques:
* **Database**: MySQL 8.0+
* **Advanced SQL**: Common Table Expressions (CTEs), Window Functions (`DENSE_RANK`, `ROW_NUMBER`, `LAG`, `LEAD`, `SUM() OVER`), Multi-table JOINs, Conditional Aggregations (`CASE`), Subqueries.
* **Data Scale**: 5,000 Customers | 50 Branches | 7,000 Accounts | 105,000 Transactions | 3,000 Loans | 79,000+ Payments | 5,200 Cards.
* **Data Quality Auditing**: 14 automated integrity scripts validating temporal logic, PK/FK constraints, and zero orphan records.

---

### 💡 Key Business Findings:
1. **Wealth Concentration (The 25/72 Skew)**: The top 25.4% of customers (**Premium segment**) hold **71.9% of total bank deposits** (~₹2.66B), highlighting the critical need for dedicated Relationship Management desks.
2. **Channel Migration**: Digital channels (**UPI, Mobile Banking, Net Banking**) account for **64.2% of total transaction volume**, while physical Branches and NEFT dominate large-ticket value settlements (handling ~57% of total monetary value).
3. **Credit Portfolio & Default Distribution**: Home Loans form the bedrock of the lending book (63.4% of capital deployed, ₹5.04B) with the lowest default rate (6.76%), whereas unsecured Personal Loans experienced elevated delinquency (9.54%).
4. **Branch Benchmarking**: Dehradun, Indore, and Aurangabad emerged as the top 3 deposit-mobilizing branches through ranked window function scorecards.
